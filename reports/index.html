📈 Revenue & Profit Trend

Business Insight
Annual revenue grew from ₹2.6M in 2022 to ₹9.5M in 2024 (+262%), tracking the growing customer base over the period. The single strongest month was 2024-12 at ₹0.9M — worth checking what drove that spike (promotion, seasonality, cohort effect) so it can be repeated deliberately.

🛒 Category Performance

Business Insight
Electronics is the single biggest revenue driver at ₹8.4M (45% of total). Clothing carries the healthiest margin (53%), while Grocery is thin at 18% — high volume there is propping up revenue, not profit. A pricing or bundling review on Grocery would move the needle more than chasing more volume in it.

👥 Customer Retention

Business Insight
On average 71% of active customers each month are returning buyers, not new ones. Excluding the very first month (no prior cohort exists yet to return from), the returning-customer share has improved (26% → 88%). Since a large share of the customer base is one-time or occasional buyers rather than loyal repeat customers, a post-first-purchase win-back campaign (email/discount within 30-45 days) would likely have more impact than acquisition spend alone.

🏆 Top Products

Business Insight
The top 3 products alone account for 52% of top-10 revenue, led by Laptop v3 (₹2496K, 1,724 units). That concentration is a supply-chain risk as much as a strength — a stockout on Laptop v3 would disproportionately hurt revenue, so it's worth prioritizing inventory buffer and supplier reliability there specifically.

🏙️ City Leaderboard

Business Insight
Mumbai leads at ₹3.5M, 238% higher than the lowest city, Ahmedabad. The top 3 cities generate 51% of total revenue — expansion marketing spend is likely better targeted at tier-2 cities like Ahmedabad or Jaipur where the ceiling hasn't been tested, rather than deepening an already-saturated top market.
